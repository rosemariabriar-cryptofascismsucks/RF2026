SCHEDULING AND TIME CLOCK POLICY HOURS AND AVAILABILITY

You will be asked to commit to update hours of

availability in Royal Farms time and attendance system.

Occasionally circumstances will require changes to

availability. Because customer service is our #1 priority,

we may ask you to alter your work schedule to meet

seasonal or other shopping patterns.

Employees must verify their time weekly in the Reflexis

Time & Attendance system. This includes hours worked

and any paid time off that has been taken. Any edits

made to your time are visible on the “Audit” tab of your

timecard. Each edit should be reviewed and

approved. If you have concerns about an edit made to

your time, you should disapprove the edit and speak with

your supervisor. Employees must sign off on the

timecard by Wednesday for the prior week. Please see

your supervisor for more information.

OFF-THE-CLOCK WORK IS NOT PERMITTED!

When using the time clock, you will use the employee

number assigned to you when hired. You will be shown

the operation and location of the time clock during your

training. Please keep the following guidelines in mind:

• Use the time clock that has been assigned to your

store.

• Use the time clock for all punches, including

meal periods.

• You must be ready to work when you punch in.

• Do not punch in or begin work before your shift

begins unless otherwise instructed by

management.

• You must punch out immediately after being

released from work.

• Any mistakes or problems should be directed to

your Supervisor.

• Do not ask anyone to key in a punch for you.

• Do not key in a punch for anyone else.

• Do not loiter in the store when your shift has ended.

MEAL PERIODS

All hourly employee of Royal Farms, to include FSLs, CSLs, Shift Leaders and ASLs will receive breaks based on the number of worked hours as follows:

• 4 hours or less – none

• 4 to 6 consecutive hours – 15-minute,

uninterrupted paid break

• 6 to 8 consecutive hours – 30-minute,

uninterrupted unpaid break

• 8 consecutive hours –

one 15-minute uninterrupted paid break

one 30-minute uninterrupted unpaid break

SHOW UP TO WORK ON TIME AND READY TO WORK

– Being ready for work means showing up for work in a clean uniform with good personal hygiene at or 5 minutes before your scheduled start time. If you need a meal before the start of your shift, this must be completed prior to clocking in.

YOUR SHIFT = YOUR RESPONSIBILITY –

Call-outs are unacceptable. Every employee must

have both a copy of the schedule and a copy of the

employee phone list. If you cannot make your shift

after the schedule has been posted, it is your

responsibility to cover your shift with a trained

replacement. All switches must be approved by a

member of the leadership team. If you ask for

assistance at least 8 hours prior to the start of your

shift, a member of leadership can assist in finding

coverage, however the shift is still your

responsibility until a suitable replacement is found.

Employees may utilize their Shift Trade Board in

Reflexis to swap shifts with other eligible

employees, advertise their shifts to other eligible

employees and also request additional shifts. The

Store Leader must approve all requests. You are still

responsible for your scheduled shift until any request

has been approved. Please see your supervisor for

more information.

BE PRODUCTIVE

The old saying applies, if

there is time to lean there is time to clean. This

translates to a standard of productivity. The

expectation is that all employees are being fully

productive the entire time that they are being paid to

Work.

AVAILABILITY

Upon hiring, you provided a

specific availability that you agreed to work, and the

company hired you based on the needs of the

business. Any changes to that availability must be

submitted and approved by the Store Leader.

Availability changes should be provided two weeks

in advance of any needed and agreed upon schedule

revisions. Any availability change that results in a

reduced work schedule or any change to weekend or

overnight availability must be accompanied by

supporting written documentation such as a school

schedule, job schedule, sports schedule, etc. Any

availability that changes the status for which you

were hired is subject to final review and approval by

the company. This change could result in a

reduction of hours and/or a reduction of pay. If you

are seeking additional hours, adjusting your

availability to the needs of the business and being

available to pick up shifts will be impactful to this

Cause.

DAYS OFF SCHEDULE REQUESTS

Schedule requests for days off, both paid and unpaid should be made through Reflexis, employee self-service. Schedules are generated 2 weeks in advance, 

  • therefore, employees are required to provide 14 days’ notice for requested days off.
  • Employees requesting paid time off must ensure that they have enough accrued leave available to cover the dates requested. All requests are subject to an approval process. Please refer to the employee handbook for information regarding paid vacation, personal time and types of leave.

STANDARDS OF CONDUCT & DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS

Royal Farms does not believe it is necessary to list exhaustively every work rule or standard of conduct for our Associates. You are expected to perform your job well, to conduct yourself in a professional manner at all times, and to treat others with honor, dignity, and respect. Additionally, all Associates are expected to comply with any rules applicable to employment with Royal Farms (including, but not limited to, the rules set forth in this Handbook).

In some instances of rule violations, improper conduct, or unsatisfactory job performance, progressive disciplinary action may be appropriate. Serious instances of rule violations, improper conduct, or unsatisfactory job performance, as determined by leadership may result

in severe forms of disciplinary action, up to and including immediate termination of employment,

without prior progressive disciplinary action. The following are among the most common examples for severe disciplinary action, including immediate

termination:

  • • Clocking another team member in or out
  • • Clocking in or out for an Associate who did not work, or otherwise submitting to be paid for time not worked.
  • • Editing an employee’s time to remove time that was actually worked or assigning an unpaid break to an employee’s record that was not taken.
  • Reporting to work under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs
  • Insubordination or disrespect to retail team Leaders
  •  Excessive unexcused tardiness or absence, or failure to notify leadership of tardiness or absences in a timely manner
  • Dishonesty or falsification of employment or other records or documents
  • Use of inappropriate language or other inappropriate behavior directed toward other employees, customers or third persons including, but not limited to
  •  profanity,
  • obscenity,
  • cursing, or
  • other foul or offensive language on company property
  • and/or loud or disruptive language, fighting, or conduct
  • Possession or use of dangerous weapons on company property
  • Cash theft which includes, but is not limited to:
  • Taking money from the cash register (which may be revealed by a cash shortage)
  • Voiding a line or transaction after the sale has taken place and the customer receives the merchandise
  • Failing to ring up a sale or a part of a Sale
  •  Purposely giving too much change
  • Violation of our Civility, Equal Employment
  • Opportunity & Non-Harassment policy
  • Food theft, which includes, but is not limited to:
  • Giving away food over the counter without receipt of the appropriate payment and/or coupons in exchange
  • Intentionally over-portioning products
  • Taking food home without payment after closing or finishing your shift
  • Storing food for later pick-up without payment
  • Signing for food or goods not delivered
